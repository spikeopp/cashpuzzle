Delete Account

Cash Puzzle : Solve & Earn · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cash Puzzle has no login or user account. Your "account" is your game data on your phone, plus any withdrawal requests you have sent. You can delete all of it as shown below.

Step 1: Delete the data on your phone

Do either of these:

  • Uninstall the App (long-press the Cash Puzzle icon → Uninstall), or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → Cash Puzzle → Storage → Clear data.

This permanently deletes your level progress, wallet balance, settings and installation ID from the device. If Android backup is on, you can also remove the app's backup in Settings → Google → Backup.

Step 2: Delete data stored on our servers

If you ever submitted a withdrawal request, we hold the details you entered. To delete them:

  1. Email growesports.com@gmail.com from the email address you used in your withdrawal request.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my account – Cash Puzzle".
  3. Include the name and payment ID (UPI ID / PayPal email / phone) you entered, so we can find your requests.

We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and payment details.
  • All withdrawal requests linked to you, including amount, country, level and installation ID.

What may be kept

  • Records of payouts we have already made, kept for up to 24 months where needed for accounting, legal or fraud-prevention reasons, then deleted.
  • Anonymous analytics held by Google Firebase (not linked to your name or email), deleted automatically under Firebase retention (up to 14 months).

Please note: deleting your account removes any remaining wallet balance and pending withdrawal requests, and this cannot be undone.
