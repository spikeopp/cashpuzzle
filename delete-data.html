Delete Data

Cash Puzzle : Solve & Earn · Last updated: 20 November 2026

You can ask us to delete some of your data without deleting everything. Because the App has no login, there are two places where data is kept.

Data on your phone

Your progress, wallet balance and settings stay on your device. To remove them, go to Settings → Apps → Cash Puzzle → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. This happens immediately.

Data on our servers

We only store data on our servers when you submit a withdrawal request. You can ask us to delete:

  • Payment details only: your UPI ID, Paytm number, PayPal email or gift-card email.
  • Contact details: your name and email address.
  • All withdrawal requests: every request linked to you.

How to request deletion

  1. Email growesports.com@gmail.com from the email address used in your withdrawal request.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my data – Cash Puzzle".
  3. Say which data you want deleted (see the list above), and include the name and payment ID you entered.

We will reply to confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Retention

Records of payouts already made may be kept for up to 24 months for accounting, legal or fraud-prevention reasons, and are then deleted. Anonymous analytics data is deleted automatically by Google Firebase (up to 14 months).

Advertising data

Ad networks use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Want to delete everything? See Delete Account.
