Privacy Policy

Cash Puzzle : Solve & Earn · Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cash Puzzle : Solve & Earn mobile app (package com.cashpuzzle.solveearn, "the App", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. No account or login

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. Your game progress is linked to your device, not to a personal account.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Game progress: current level, puzzle board, moves and hints.
  • Your in-app wallet balance and pending rewards.
  • Settings such as sound and vibration.
  • The country/currency selected for your wallet (detected once from your SIM card, mobile network or device language).
  • A random installation ID created by the App.

This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google backup.

3. Information you give us in a withdrawal request

When you submit a withdrawal request in the App, we collect:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your chosen payment method and payment details (for example a UPI ID, Paytm number, PayPal email or an email address for a gift card).
  • The amount requested, currency, country, wallet balance, level reached, app version, installation ID and the time of the request.

This information is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is used only to verify and process your payout, to contact you about it, and to prevent fraud and duplicate requests.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress.
  • To show ads, which fund the App and its rewards.
  • To process withdrawal requests and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To understand and improve the App.
  • To reply when you contact us.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, with payment or gift-card providers when sending your payout, or when required by law.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Withdrawal requests: for up to 24 months after the request is processed, for accounting and fraud prevention, and then deleted.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google according to Firebase retention settings (up to 14 months).

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your withdrawal-request data. See Delete Data and Delete Account.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Withdrawal requests can only be created by the App and cannot be read from it; access is limited to the App's administrators.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Withdrawal requests must be made by users aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information on servers outside your country. They protect it under their own safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
